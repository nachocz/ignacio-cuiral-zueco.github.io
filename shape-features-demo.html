What changes between representations?

Point coordinates

Keep position, orientation and shape. Best for placement, but any rigid motion shows up as error.

Edge vectors

Remove translation while keeping orientation and local direction changes. Translating the object leaves this error untouched.

Curvature

Remove translation and rotation, isolating intrinsic bending. Shape converges, but pose becomes unobservable and uncontrolled.
